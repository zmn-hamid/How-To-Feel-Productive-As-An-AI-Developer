# How To Feel Productive As An AI Developer

AI development made me feel empty. I was struggling with feeling productive doing any AI development. After a good while, I can finally be a little more confident in what helps overcoming this feeling. It's not a manifesto for or against AI development. I'm not here to tell you it's the future or that it's theft. I'm just a developer who got forced into this workflow, struggled with it, found a few things that helped, and wrote them down.

If you've ever felt exhausted after a day of "managing" an agent, or unproductive because you spent eight hours waiting for a prompt to finish, or hollow because you can't point at anything and say "I made that", this might resonate... hopefully so.

The article covers the psychological and the practical techniques I've found that helped me immesnsly with my journey. I'd be glad to know your opinion and techniques dealing with these issues. I'd probably add them to the list too if I can confidently tell them to other people. So please feel free to email me or open a discussion / issue here.
